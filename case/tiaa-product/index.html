From Trust to Advice: Wealth Experience for the $1.5T Retirement Giant

Five million people trust TIAA with their retirement, but few think of it for wealth advice. I led product strategy and design for the wealth journeys that close that gap, backed by research and experiments at every step.

The business problem

TIAA is the largest provider of retirement plans in the US non-profit sector, and it is aggressively growing its wealth management business: more than $200B in assets under management and about 700 financial advisors, with a strategy to double advisor headcount over five years.

Trusted for retirement, lacking visibility for wealth

More advisors only grow the business if qualified clients reach them. But prospects were building careers, families and wealth without knowing TIAA offered wealth services, and those who did found onboarding slow and hard to finish.

My role

I owned the wealth journeys end to end: what to build and why, how we’d know it worked, and getting the teams who deliver it aligned behind it.

Product strategy and wealth journeys

Led end-to-end product strategy and the design of the wealth journeys, measured by business growth: conversion, retention and assets under management.

Research and experimentation strategy

Defined and ran a structured research and testing framework, so concepts and assumptions were validated before they were built.

AI exploration and personalization

Defined AI concepts and judged them by their impact, putting augmentation of human advisors ahead of low-value automation.

Cross-functional alignment and delivery

Drove alignment across Product, Data, Engineering and the advisors, to deliver measurable improvements.

Barriers to wealth advisory services

  1. Disconnect on investing values
  2. Not understanding the cost (fees) / benefit
  3. Expertise not shown beyond their own skill set
  4. Loss of sense of control
  5. Lacking motivation for “more money”
  6. Lack of trust

Needs of an advised relationship are also different based on life stage

Advisor relationships are central to a positive Wealth client experience.

  • Many participants talk about a longstanding relationship with a specific person.
  • Participants also share impacts of negative advisor experiences, underscoring their importance.
  • A consistent, supportive relationship with an advisor is enormously important.

A live chat with an AI-powered Virtual Advisory Assistant came out on bottom as the solution least likely to enhance TIAA’s Wealth client experience.

Competitive Analysis

While we were doing research with the users, we were also doing competitive analysis.

Key Insights

Income-first is conversion lever

Lead with guaranteed lifetime-income outcomes (not balances) to translate retirement trust into wealth relationships.

Advisor continuity wins retention

Documented handoffs, meeting summaries and proactive follow-ups materially increase client trust and downstream asset transfers.

Low-friction accelerates bookings

A 3-step digital intake that prepopulates advisor briefs and connects external accounts cuts time-to-book and drop-offs.

Personalization across lifecycle

Tailor content and CTAs by segment (prospect → unassigned → assigned → engaged) to prevent irrelevant, one-size messaging.

Data integration is table stakes

Aggregation (Yodlee), MGP & Salesforce syncs are prerequisites for real-time, actionable advice and dynamic nudges.

In summary

An income-first, omni-channel experience, built on TIAA’s guaranteed-income product and validated through structured research and testing, turned plan participants into wealth clients: ↑18% prospect conversion.

By augmenting human advisors rather than automating them away, the experience kept the relationship at its centre, with ↑40% advisor-digital continuity and ↑45% dynamic advice engagement.
