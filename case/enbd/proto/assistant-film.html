How it's put together

  • Each segment is its own file, unchanged: the intro, the three feature beats and the outro. Its CSS, markup and script are lifted in whole, then scoped to its layer — selectors prefixed, keyframes renamed — so identically named animations from different files can't collide.
  • Each script runs in its own factory. Its $ only searches its own layer, and its loop is replaced by two hooks: exit (its own exit starts) and end (its exit has played out). The conductor below listens to those and nothing else.
  • So tuning anything still happens in its own file, and this film is rebuilt from the five with build_film.py. The features reel is built separately and isn't touched by it.
  • The outro hands off at its end, not at its exit: its exit is only the words leaving, with the orb and then the home screen still to come.

Order: intro → spending breakdown → dining offers → transfer status → outro, ending on the home screen. Keys: space play, 1–5 jump to a segment, T transition, R replay the current segment.
